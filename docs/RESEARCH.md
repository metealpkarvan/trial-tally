The thread examines a historical trial purchase flow where billing cadence and contractual commitment were easy to confuse. It is not used as a statement about today’s pricing, terms or legal rights.

## Product inference

Saving a trial-end date is not enough when the cancellation route, commitment and confirmation receipt are still unclear. Waiting until the last day leaves no room to investigate.

Our hypothesis: A practical exit kit: an earlier decision date, separately recorded billing and commitment, a cancellation route, evidence checklist and calendar export.

The author did not request this app and has not endorsed it. Dates distinguish historical examples from current claims. No follower, revenue, effectiveness or error-rate claims are inferred.
